# Real-Time Notification Center with Preferences in Wasp.sh ## Background In modern web applications, real-time notifications are essential for keeping users engaged and informed. However, users must also have granular control over what notifications they receive to prevent alert fatigue. Wasp.sh (v0.24.0) provides built-in support for Socket.IO-based WebSockets alongside its full-stack Query/Action architecture.
